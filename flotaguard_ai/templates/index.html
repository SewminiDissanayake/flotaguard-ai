The operational problem

Quality intelligence arrives after the process has already moved.

Flotation conditions change continuously. FlotaGuard AI is designed as a predictive decision-support layer: it combines the current process state with recent silica history, forecasts the next quality window, and highlights when operator attention may be needed.
